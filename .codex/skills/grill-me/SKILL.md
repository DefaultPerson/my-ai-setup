---
name: grill-me
description: Interview the user to stress-test a plan, design or decision, resolve dependencies and expose assumptions before implementation. Use when asked to grill, interview, challenge or stress-test a plan, in any language.
---

Map the decision tree and its dependencies. In each round, ask the current frontier: all unresolved decisions whose prerequisites are already settled. Number the questions, give a concise recommended answer for each, and make “yes” accept that recommendation. Wait for the answers before asking dependent questions.

Research facts you can establish from available code, documents or tools; do not ask the user to do that work. Keep independent questions moving while research runs when supported. Decisions about goals, tradeoffs and preferences remain with the user; do not silently answer them.

Update the tree after every answer, challenge contradictions and surface downstream consequences. Continue until every relevant branch is settled or explicitly deferred. Summarize the agreed understanding and remaining deferrals; wait for confirmation before implementation or changing files.
